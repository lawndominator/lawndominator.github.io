Best lawn care app for Bermuda grass

The best Bermuda app is not just a reminder app. It needs warm-season timing, product logging, GDD, soil temperature, and PGR history.

What Bermuda users need

  • Green-up and soil-temperature awareness.
  • Pre-emergent timing support before crabgrass germination.
  • Fertilizer and spray calculations by lawn size.
  • PGR interval tracking using GDD from the last application date.
  • Photos to compare scalping, recovery, color, and density.

Why Lawn Dominator fits

Lawn Dominator connects the Bermuda calendar to tools that matter in the field: soil temperature, GDD, spray logs, fertilizer logs, tank mix planning, and product history.
