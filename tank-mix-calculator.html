Tank mix calculator for lawn care

A tank mix plan should calculate amounts, respect labels, check compatibility, and leave a record of what went into the sprayer.

Start with the label

Extension pesticide safety guidance is consistent: the label controls use. If the label prohibits a mixture, rate, turf species, site, or condition, the calculator does not make it safe.

Compatibility matters

Montana State Extension and Nebraska Extension both describe jar tests as a way to check physical compatibility before mixing pesticides at tank scale. This is especially important when labels do not provide clear tank-mix instructions or when water quality may affect the mix.

What Lawn Dominator helps track

  • Products and rates by lawn size.
  • Carrier volume and spray notes.
  • Weather and timing conditions.
  • Application history for follow-up decisions.
