Why the calendar is only the start

Extension calendars are regional planning tools. NC State notes that location, terrain, soil type, lawn age, previous care, and other factors affect turf performance, so dates need local adjustment. Lawn Dominator adds the local timing layer: soil temperature, GDD, weather, treatment logs, and photos.

Use the tools with the calendar

  • Soil Temperature Tracker for pre-emergent, green-up, and disease-timing decisions.
  • GDD Calculator for seasonal heat accumulation and PGR intervals.
  • Lawn Treatment Tracker to turn the calendar into a real application history.
