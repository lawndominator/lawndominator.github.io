Spray log app for lawn care

A good spray log records more than the product name. It captures rate, lawn size, water volume, weather, timing signal, and what happened afterward.

Save a spray log in this browser

What to log

  • Product, active ingredient, rate, and treated area.
  • Carrier volume, nozzle/sprayer notes, and tank mix order.
  • Weather, soil temperature, GDD, and stress conditions.
  • Photos and follow-up notes 7 to 21 days later.

Why it matters

Turf response depends on timing, weather, product fit, and stress. Without a spray log, you lose the context needed to repeat good applications and avoid bad ones.

Pair the spray log with the tank mix calculator guide, soil temperature tracker, and GDD calculator.
