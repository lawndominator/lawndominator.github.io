Who, how, why

About Lawn Dominator

Lawn Dominator exists because serious lawn care gets messy fast: products, rates, weather, soil temperature, GDD, photos, and follow-up notes all need one reliable place.

What we build

Lawn Dominator is a lawn tracker app for fertilizer logs, spray logs, tank mix planning, soil temperature, GDD, diagnostics, product history, and seasonal treatment planning.

How content is created

Our research pages use university extension and turf program sources wherever possible. We avoid unsupported treatment promises and keep label cautions visible because pesticide and fertilizer use depends on product, turf species, site, weather, and local rules.

Why the app exists

The goal is not to replace labels, soil tests, or local extension guidance. The goal is to help homeowners organize the decisions they already need to make and keep a record of what happened.
