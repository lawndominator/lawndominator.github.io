Comparison

Lawn Dominator vs spreadsheet

A spreadsheet can store rows. Lawn Dominator is built around lawn decisions: timing, calculations, products, photos, and treatment history.

Where spreadsheets work

Spreadsheets are flexible and familiar. They can be fine if you only want a date, product, and note field.

Where Lawn Dominator wins

  • Built-in lawn treatment flow instead of blank rows.
  • Soil temperature and GDD tools tied to timing decisions.
  • Product library, spray logs, fertilizer logs, and photos in one place.
  • Mobile-first logging while you are actually working outside.

Best fit

Use a spreadsheet if you want a blank database. Use Lawn Dominator if you want a lawn-specific operating system for fertilizer, spray, timing, and results.
